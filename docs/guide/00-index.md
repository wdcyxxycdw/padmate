# padmate user guide

[中文](00-index.zh-CN.md)

padmate turns the buttons, sticks and triggers of a controller into keyboard shortcuts. The typical use is painting with the controller in your left hand and the pen in your right.

padmate works with Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro and Joy-Con controllers, including the Joy-Con 2 of the Switch 2. Each controller has its own function key, feedback and emergency pause.

Words in quotation marks are the exact text of the interface; the tray menu is in Chinese only. The interface starts in the language you chose when installing; to change it, open “Settings”, go to “General” and change “Language”.
